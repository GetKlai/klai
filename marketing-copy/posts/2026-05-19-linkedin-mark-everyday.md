## Copy

**Body.**

I built Klai because I watched smart people ask stupid questions. Not the questions themselves-the *friction* around asking them.

A product manager wanting to check the client agreement. A developer needing the internal playbook. Someone looking up a project spec. Each time, a pause. A Slack to legal. A day or two of waiting. The assumption that asking an AI anything confidential meant breaking policy.

We spent months building EU data governance and contractual guarantees into the foundation precisely so that pause doesn't have to exist. No data training on your documents. Encrypted, auditable, legally structured. The compliance officer and the team member both get what they need.

That's not a feature. That's the whole thing. When compliance stops being a gate and starts being your actual operating system, work moves differently. Trust lives in the contract, not the friction.

We're seeing it now. Teams asking their AI about the things that actually matter, without the legal weight. That moment-that's why we built this.
