**Body.**

Most AI vendors won't show you how their models work. They call it proprietary. I call it a liability you're inheriting.

At Klai, we chose the opposite path: *open source*. Not as marketing. As structure. Every line of code, every decision the model makes, sits in a public repository. You can read it. Audit it. Verify it. That's not transparency theater. That's contractual accountability built into the product itself.

When regulators ask you to explain your AI, you shouldn't have to call a vendor and hope they respond. You should be able to point to the code. Point to the training data. Point to the legal foundation underneath.

We exist because AI governance isn't optional anymore. It's existential. And existential problems need existential solutions, not closed systems and vendor promises.

If your compliance officer is losing sleep over how your AI actually works, we built Klai for that exact conversation.
