# Transparent pricing. EUR 28/mo or EUR 68/mo. That's it.

- **Post ID:** `2026-05-29-x-pricing`
- **Date:** 2026-05-29
- **Channel/format:** x / square (1200x1200)
- **Voice:** brand
- **Narrative role:** proof

## Intent
Pricing as part of the transparency story. No surprise sub-processors, no hidden costs, no enterprise seat-licensing games. Simple, clear, fair.

## Visual brief
**Concept.** A single stat card presenting the two transparent pricing tiers as a proof point of Klai's commitment to simplicity and fairness. The visual emphasizes pricing clarity as structural differentiation, not feature abundance. Warm off-white field, black typography, klai wordmark anchoring the top. The two prices (EUR 28/mo, EUR 68/mo) are the hero, rendered as a clean numerical statement with minimal caption below. The feeling is calm authority: pricing so simple it needs no explanation, no hidden layers, no confusion. This is editorial clarity in contrast to industry opacity.

**Composition.** Warm off-white background, klai wordmark small and centred at top. Two prices displayed prominently in the mid-frame as the focal point: "28 / 68" in huge inter Medium black type with tight tracking, each price preceded by the EUR symbol. Below the prices, a single-line caption in muted ink reads "EUR per month. No surprises." All elements vertically centred with breathing room above and below. Generous left and right margins. No CTA pill, no terminal strip, no secondary imagery.

**Palette.** #000000, #FFFFFF, #F5F3F0
**Typography.** Bold geometric sans-serif (Inter Medium), huge scale with tight tracking for the two prices. Subtitle in light sans-serif body copy, muted ink black. Klai wordmark small at top in brand weight. All lowercase, no italics on this stat card (italics reserved for body copy emphasis).
**Style.** editorial, minimalist, type-driven, calm authority, publication aesthetic

**Text overlay.** 28 / 68

**QA verdict.** approved (9/10)
