# Klai Launch Month: Your AI. Your Data. Your Rules.

## Narrative arc
Week 1 plants the flag with steward-ownership and legal structure as the core differentiator that competitors cannot replicate. Week 2 shifts to product depth and the everyday user benefit: AI that knows your documents, cited sources, connectors that sync your actual work. Week 3 directly compares against ChatGPT Enterprise and Copilot on CLOUD Act exposure, training practices, and transparency. Week 4 brings social proof via early-adopter stories and industry-specific use cases, then closes with urgency around the first-100 cap and second-cohort priming.

## Voice
Editorial, principled, quiet authority. Founder voice used where natural on compliance ethics and structural differentiation. Italics for accent emphasis, never exclamation marks or hype adjectives. Pair idealism with legal/technical specifics, not vibes. Confidence earned by sounding less loud than competitors, not more. Avoid "AI-powered," "revolutionise," marketing hype. The legal fact IS the marketing.
